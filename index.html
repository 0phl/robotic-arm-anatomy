Quick guide

Explore the model
Drag to orbit. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Right-drag or use two fingers to pan. A focused canvas also accepts the arrow keys, +, −, and Home.
Identify a component
Select its surface, its numbered marker, or its card in the component index. Choose Listen to hear the explanation, or isolate it for a closer view.
Experiment and check
Use the motion lab to move individual joints. In the knowledge check, identify five components on the model or in the index.
